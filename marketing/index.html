The Seven Phases

One booking ritual, wired end to end

Every inquiry moves through the same seven phases a real shop uses — no lead falls between the DMs and the deposit.

Each phase knows what came before it. An estimate can't go out without an assigned artist. A session can't be booked without a deposit on file. The waiver can't be skipped at check-in.

What's Inside

Everything the front desk touches

Public intake forms

Per-studio intake pages with reference photo uploads. Prospects fill them out from any link you drop in a DM — no account, no app download.

Estimates with real responses

Artists approve or decline with a price and time range. Clients respond from a secure link — proceed, budget concerns, or pass — and you see when it's been opened.

Conflict-aware scheduling

Drag-and-drop calendar by artist, buffer warnings between sessions, and a waitlist for the dates that fill up.

Deposits that hold their value

Collected online like gift cards, applied at checkout, redeemed or rolled per card. A paid deposit is what turns an inquiry into a booked project.

Day-of waivers, done right

ID verification, health screening, and clause-by-clause initials at the front desk — signed before the needle touches skin.

Every conversation, threaded

Client threads tagged by channel — SMS, email, Instagram, Facebook, phone — plus templates and AI-drafted intake from conversation history.

Zero Friction

Your clients never make an account

Every step a client takes happens through a secure, single-use link — sent by text or email, opened on their phone, done in a minute. No passwords, no downloads.

Links expire, tokens are invalidated after use, and the studio sees exactly where each person is in the process.
